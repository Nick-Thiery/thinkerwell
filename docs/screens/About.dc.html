About Thinkerwell

Read with curiosity. Write with evidence. Speak with purpose. Watch with intention. Reflect and connect.

What Thinkerwell is

Thinkerwell is a free social-studies course for young people across Southeast Asia, made with refugee, displaced and under-served learners in mind. Learners explore history, geography, culture and civic life through reading, writing, talking and reflecting.

It is a student-led project, getting ready for its first pilot with a learning centre. It is not a registered charity.

Our promise to learners

It is free, with no adverts and no accounts. We never ask about your past, your family or where you come from. Your writing stays on your device. You choose what to share.

The UN goals we work towards

Goal 4: Quality EducationFree social-studies learning for young people who face barriers to school.
Goal 10: Reduced InequalitiesGood learning materials for refugee, displaced and under-served youth.
Goal 16: Peace, Justice and Strong InstitutionsHistory, civics and media skills, and the habit of respectful discussion.
Goal 17: Partnerships for the GoalsWorking with educators, youth networks and community groups.
