Geography & Our Environment
Lesson 10

Why do people build towns near rivers?

What makes one place better than another for a community to live?

Talk about the place you chose for your town. You can record yourself if you like. Recordings stay on this device.

With a partner

Share your choice with a partner. Explain your two reasons. Then ask your partner: "How would your town deal with its biggest problem?"

On your own

Say your answer out loud, or quietly to yourself, in three sentences. Then add one sentence about a problem your town may face.

Say it in three sentences

  1. I would build my town at the ___.
  2. One reason is ___. Another reason is ___.
  3. One problem could be ___.

Say your three sentences, then listen back. Did you give two reasons? Try again as many times as you like.
