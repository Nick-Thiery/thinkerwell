Lesson 10

Why do people build towns near rivers?

What makes one place better than another for a community to live?

Listen Key words

Look at the map. Where would you build a new town? Guess now. You can change your mind later.

Near the river On the hill In the forest
Rivers can bring problems too. After a lot of rain, a river can flood. Water covers fields, roads and homes for a time. People find ways to get ready. Some families build houses on tall legs. Then water can go under the house. Some towns build high walls of earth by the river. Other towns are on a hill. But then people carry water a long way. Every place has good things and risks.
