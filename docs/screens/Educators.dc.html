For teachers and volunteers

Run a Thinkerwell lesson with your group

Every lesson works on one shared screen, in pairs, or on separate devices. There are no accounts to set up.

Preview a lesson Lesson plans (PDF)

What you need

One device for every 1 to 3 learners, or one big screen Paper and pencils for planning About 30 minutes a lesson Internet only for videos, once a lesson has opened

How a session works

  1. 1Pick a lessonLessons can go in any order. Choose one that fits your group, or follow the numbers.
  2. 2Work through five stepsRead, Write, Speak, Watch, Reflect. Pair learners for Speak. Watch can be skipped.
  3. 3Share and reflectAsk two or three learners to share their answer. Everyone writes one reflection.

Pilot partners

Class codes instead of names

In the pilot, each learner gets a code like HLP-07. Codes let us see how the whole group is doing without any names. You keep the list that matches names to codes.
