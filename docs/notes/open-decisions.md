# Open decisions before the pilots

For Nick and Justin, 20 November 2026, before the pilots with HELP for Refugees (Jakarta) and the partner in Malaysia. Two lists: the decisions `docs/PRODUCT.md` still leaves open, and the teachers' sensitive notes (`sensitiveNotes` in the lesson files) that ask for someone to review a lesson before it is taught. Each has a recommendation; **nothing here has been changed in the site**. When one is decided, change `docs/PRODUCT.md` (and `CLAUDE.md` if it is a rule) in the same pull request, and strike it here.

## 2. Sensitive notes that ask for a review before teaching

These come from the lessons' `sensitiveNotes`, and from the course READMEs where they name a partner review. The teacher guides show each lesson's notes first, marked (`/educators/lesson/<id>`).

### Review by a local educator before the lesson is used

| Lesson | What the note asks | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exploring Our World, Lesson 4: How do people explain where the world comes from?** | A local educator, ideally from the learners' own communities, reviews it first: most learners are likely from Muslim-majority communities, and others hold different beliefs or none. | **HELP:** a HELP educator reads it, in Indonesian if that is what learners will use, before it is scheduled. Don't put it in the first weeks. **Malaysia:** the Malaysian reviewer reads the Malay version with particular care (`PRODUCT.md`), since religion is a sensitive subject in Malaysian schools. |
| **Exploring Our World, Lesson 19: What does belonging mean?** | A local educator reviews it first: belonging can bring up feelings about home, family or loss. Preview its video too. | **Both pilots:** reviewed by the person who will teach it, with the session guide's safeguarding lines at hand. Teach it after learners know the routine, not early. |

The session guide already tells staff that Lessons 4 and 19 are reviewed by a local educator first.

### Review by the partner organisation (named in the course READMEs)

| Course and lessons | Topics | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
