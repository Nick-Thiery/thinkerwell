# Pilot-day tools

Branch `pilot-day-tools`. Built on 28 September 2026.

On pilot day, HELP's staff set up shared laptops and tablets and follow their group without the founders in the room. Three pages under Educators help them do that. All three work offline, read only what is on the device, and send nothing anywhere.

## What was built

- **Set up this device** at `/educators/setup` (`src/pages/educators/SetupPage.tsx`). A checklist to work through once on each device. Each step says whether it is done on this device, where the app can know:
  1. **Add Thinkerwell to the home screen.** Done when Thinkerwell runs as an installed app (`display-mode: standalone`, `fullscreen` or `minimal-ui`, or Safari's `navigator.standalone`). Short steps for iPad or iPhone (Safari), Android (Chrome), and Windows laptops or Chromebooks (Chrome or Edge). The steps for this kind of device come first, from the user agent (an iPad that asks for the desktop site is told apart from a Mac by its touch screen). The others are behind "Steps for other devices".
  2. **Download the course for offline use.** The same states and words as Settings' "Offline and data" (`src/pages/settings/offlineStatus.ts`, shared by both): done, in progress, checking, didn't finish, or not possible in this browser.
  3. **Keep saved work safe.** Whether storage is persistent (`navigator.storage.persisted()`). "Keep work safe" calls `navigator.storage.persist()` on the tap, and says if the browser says no.
  4. **Add the learners who use this device.** How many learners there are, "Add a learner" (clears the current learner and opens the new-learner form, as the header's "I'm new here" does) and "See the class".
  5. **Choose the Listen voice (optional)**, added in October 2026 (`docs/notes/listen-voices.md`). Which voice Listen reads with on this device, the best one or the one chosen in Settings, and "Choose it in Settings", which goes to `/settings#listen-voice`. "Optional" until a voice is chosen, then done; "Not on this browser" where the device has no voice Listen can use. It reads the voice list (`getVoices()`), which asks the browser for nothing, and speaks nothing. On paper it has its box, title and what to do, but not its reason, and the steps are 3 mm apart (3.5 mm before), so the checklist still fits one A4 page in Indonesian (on US Letter paper, Indonesian now takes two pages; English still takes one, and Indonesia uses A4).
  6. **Check speech to text.** The saved result of Settings' "Check this device" (`settings.speechCheck`), with its date, and "Check it in Settings", which goes to `/settings#say-it`.
  - Then **Before you reset this device or give it back**: save everyone's work to a file, with a link to `/settings#move-work`.
- **The class on this device** at `/educators/class` (`ClassPage.tsx`, numbers in `classSummary.ts`). Every learner on the device, by name. Each card shows the lessons finished in each section ("5 of 9"), the lesson they are on now and its step (or the lesson up next), the day they last worked, and which section checks they have tried. It shows everyone while someone is looking around too: like Settings, it is about the device.
- **All certificates** at `/educators/class/certificates` (`AllCertificatesPage.tsx`), from the class view's "Print all certificates". Every certificate earned on this device: each learner's finished sections, then the course certificate if earned, learners by name. Each prints on a landscape page of its own, in the layout of a learner's own certificate.
- **The pilot day guide for staff** at `/educators/session-guide` (`PilotDayPage.tsx`), added in October 2026 (branch `staff-one-pager`). One page, for screen and paper, for the adults running a session at any partner organisation: it names no organisation and no place, and says nothing about measurement beyond what the consent form says (`pages.pilotStudy`). Six parts, each a short list: **Before the session** (set up each device, print the code cards, check each family has the information sheet and has answered the consent form, a learner's own no wins); **Start of the session** (the home page's "Who's learning today?", new learners tap "I'm new here", "Just look around" is for visitors and saves nothing); **What learners do** (the five stages, nothing locked, any order, about how long a lesson takes: the same range as the Educators page, and the teacher guides' 45 and 30 minute plans); **If something goes wrong** (a page that hasn't downloaded: Try again; no internet: keep going, work saves on the device; several learners on one device: each taps their own tile; work that seems missing: load the saved file in Settings, found in the device's downloads with a name starting `thinkerwell`; a video that won't play: Read instead; no sound or no Say it button: normal, they show only where the device can do them); **Sensitive topics** (never ask about journey, home country, family, religion or ethnicity; read each teacher guide's "Sensitive topics: read before class" first; Lessons 4 and 19 ask for a local educator to review them first; thank and move on if a learner shares something personal, and follow the organisation's safeguarding steps if worried); **End of the session** (save the work to a file before a device is reset or given back, print certificates for finished sections, see the class). The buttons and pages it names come from the app's own messages (`nav.educators`, `pages.setup.title`, `pageNotDownloaded.retry` and so on), set in bold, so a label that changes changes here too in every language. **Who to contact** is shown only once `CONTACT_EMAIL` is set (`src/app/contact.ts`), with the Educators page's own "Tell us what to fix" words; until then the page has no such line and no placeholder.
- **The Educators page** has a new part, "Set up devices and follow your group", between the introduction and "How a session works", with a row for each page. The rows look like a section's check and answer key under the lesson list.
- **Settings** now has addresses of its own: `/settings#say-it` and `/settings#move-work` (`settingsPath()` in `src/app/lessonUrls.ts`), and since October 2026 `/settings#listen-voice`. Opened with one, the page scrolls to that part and moves focus to its heading once the settings and learners are read.

## Decisions

1. **The home screen step comes first, and the speech check last.** The task listed the download first. On iPad and iPhone, a Home Screen web app keeps its own saved work, apart from Safari: learners added in Safari would not appear in the Home Screen app. So staff add Thinkerwell to the home screen first and do everything else from there. Chrome is also more likely to grant persistent storage to an installed app, so "Keep saved work safe" comes after it. The speech check is last because asking about speech recognition has crashed the tab on touch devices (`src/speech/recognition.ts`); if it does, everything else is already done. The page says both in one plain line.
2. **Nothing risky as the page opens.** It reads the service worker's state, `persisted()` (which never prompts), the display mode, the user agent and the saved settings. `persist()` runs only on the tap. The page never calls a SpeechRecognition API; it only checks whether the browser has one at all, as Settings does, to say "Not needed" where there is nothing to check. `src/pages/speechOnOpen.test.tsx` now opens it too.
3. **"Download the course" has no button.** Settings has none either: the course downloads by itself after the first visit, and a failed download is tried again the next time Thinkerwell opens. The step shows Settings' own words, which say what to do.
4. **A speech check counts as done whatever it found.** The step is to check; lessons then know what to do. "It needs a one-time download first" shows as in progress, with the way to do it in Settings.
5. **On paper, one A4 page.** The same page prints as a checklist: a box to tick for each step, a line for the device and the date, the home screen steps for all three kinds of device side by side, and none of the states or buttons. `.tw-print-only` (in `src/pages/print/print.css`) marks what shows only on paper.
6. **No scores and no ranking in the class view.** Any learner on a shared device can open it, and the course has no leaderboards. Learners are sorted by name (ignoring case and accents); learners who share a name keep the order they were added in and show the day each was added. Section checks show only whether they were tried.
7. **"On now"** is worked out as the learner home's "Continue" is (`findContinueTarget`): the unfinished lesson worked on most recently, with its step, or, with nothing unfinished open, the next lesson up. "Last active" is the latest save of any lesson or section check.
8. **No links to journals.** The journal always shows the learner using the device now, and there is no way to open another learner's journal without switching to them. The class view never switches who is learning, so it doesn't link there.
9. **Certificates reuse the certificate itself.** The sheet moved into `src/pages/certificate/Certificate.tsx`, with its ids from `useId` and its heading level as a prop (h1 on a learner's own page, h2 here), so any number can share a page. Each one after the first starts a new page (`break-before: page`), and they all use the named landscape page (`@page tw-certificate`); the heading and notes above them never print, so the first page is a certificate too.
10. **Names can't be changed in "All certificates".** They are the names on this device. The page says so and how: choose that learner, then open their certificate from the course page, where the name can be changed for one print. That page shows the learner using the device now, so there is no direct link for each learner.
11. **Reading the whole device** uses `store.exportWork()`, the same single read as "Save my work to a file" (`src/pages/educators/useClassWork.ts`). No new stored data and no change to `DB_VERSION`.
12. **Loaded with the other Educators pages.** On this branch alone they weren't lazy-loaded, like the teacher tools then. Merged after `slow-internet` (`docs/notes/slow-internet.md`), where every page but home, the course map and the 404 loads when opened and index.html's header bar stays until it has, they are in the `teacherPages` chunk with the Educators page, the teacher guides and the answer keys: precached with the rest, opened offline (`e2e/offline.spec.ts`), started at once by the first-page script on a first visit straight to one, and loaded from the offline copy while idle like the others. The certificate sheet they share with a learner's own certificate page is a small chunk of its own (`CertificatePage-`).
13. **A new icon**, `ListChecks` (Lucide), for the checklist. It isn't in the design-system list, like `Award`.
14. **The pilot day guide's words are a lazy group, `pilotDay`** (`src/i18n/lazyGroups.ts`), so its 38 messages reach neither a first visit nor the locale chunks: they load with the Educators pages' chunk, in English, Indonesian and Malay at once, like `siteVideo`'s. `withWords` (`src/i18n/words.tsx`) now lets `tx` fall back to the app's own messages for a key the language's own words lack, as `t` already did: without it, the guide's messages with bold names in them showed plain English in the test languages (`e2e/languages.spec.ts` caught it). Three messages for its row on the Educators page (`pages.educators.pilotDay*`) are in the app's messages, because that page's other rows are.
15. **It is on the consent form's sheet**, in the Educators chunk: the same screen layout (a toolbar, a title and a line on screen, then the sheet), its parts flowing in two columns, 12 mm margins on a named page (`@page tw-consent`) and 10.5pt text, the smallest the site prints. `PilotDayPage.css` only gives its lists a little more room, because it has fewer words than the form. It has no fields to fill in (it names no organisation), and no staff note.
16. **Lessons 4 and 19 are named in code** (`REVIEW_FIRST_LESSONS` in `PilotDayPage.tsx`), because their teacher guides' first sensitive note asks for a local educator's review and no field in the lesson files says so. `PilotDayPage.test.tsx` fails if the lesson files change so that other lessons ask for a review, or these two stop.
17. **Linked from the Educators page** (and For organisations' "Get ready"), under "Resources for your group" since October 2026, when the page stopped saying "Starting a pilot" and the guide became the **session guide** at `/educators/session-guide` (the old address still opens it); after the setup checklist. The footer links only For organisations and Credits (not the partner kit's other pages), so it has no link there either. It isn't in the sitemap or the search engines' pages: it is not public (`noindex` through `app.html`, like the other tools).
18. **Words for Bahasa Indonesia and Bahasa Melayu**, like the rest: drafted by AI, flagged for the native reviewers in `docs/translation/id/notes/ui.json` and `docs/translation/ms/notes/ui.json` (41 strings each). The back-translations there were written by the drafter from the translation alone, not by a second AI that never saw the English, so a reviewer should check these rows with a little more care than the rows reviewed twice. Indonesian speaks to staff as "Anda", Malay as "anda", as on the other pages for adults; the buttons and pages it names are Settings' and the app's own words, so they stay in step with them. Vietnamese isn't translated for it (the language is hidden, and its 9 missing messages were already missing; it now misses 50).

## Sizes

On this branch alone, measured with `npm run build && npm run size` against `main`: the app code grew by 6.1 kB gzipped (the three pages and their words) and the styles by 1.1 kB; first load of Lesson 10 Read 380.9 kB gzipped (373.6 kB on `main` on the same machine), and the precache 578.4 kB (571.1 kB).

Merged with `slow-internet` and the language groundwork, measured with `npm run slow-internet -- --bytes` (Brotli): the three pages add 7.4 kB to the precache (472.4 to 479.8 kB: `teacherPages` 4.1 kB, the shared certificate chunk, their words in en.json 1.7 kB and their styles 0.9 kB) and 2.7 kB to a first visit to the home page (207.2 to 209.9 kB: their words and styles, which every page carries, since all interface words are in en.json and there is one stylesheet). Their own code never loads on a first visit to home. `docs/notes/slow-internet.md` has the combined numbers and the budget.

The pilot day guide (October 2026): the precache grew by about 4.2 kB Brotli (791.3 to 795.5 kB: the page's code about 0.7 kB, its 38 messages in three languages about 3.2 kB, the Educators page's row 0.3 kB), which took it over its 795 kB budget; the budget is now 800 kB, with the cause written down in `docs/notes/slow-internet.md`. A first visit to the home page didn't grow (223.0 kB, 223.1 kB before).

## Left for later

- **Try it on HELP's devices.** Nothing here was tried on an iPad, an Android tablet or a Chromebook. The home screen steps follow each browser's menus as of September 2026, and menus move: check the words on the real devices. Whether `persist()` says yes depends on the browser; Safari's and Chrome's answers weren't seen here.
- **Print on HELP's printer**, like the certificates: the checklist and the certificates were checked with Chromium's PDF output only.
- **A printable class list** (for a teacher's records) wasn't asked for. The class view prints with the site's plain print rules, but wasn't designed for paper.
- **Changing a name for every certificate at once** would need a name field on this page. It was left out on purpose: the names come from the device.
- **Have the guide read by someone who runs a session.** It was written from the pages as they are, not tried with staff. Whether a page is too short or too long, and whether the order of the day is the one staff follow, is theirs to say.
- **Native review of its Indonesian and Malay** (see decision 18), and a fresh review spreadsheet (`npm run review:export`, `npm run review:export -- ms`) so the new rows reach the reviewers.
