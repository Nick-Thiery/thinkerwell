# Fixes, 27 September 2026

A fresh-eyes pass over the live site (branch `about-and-fixes`), as a learner, a volunteer teacher and a partner organisation would use it. Every page was walked in a production build at 390, 820 and 1280px wide and looked at in screenshots: home (empty, with learners, looking around), the new-learner form, the learner home, the course, every stage of Lessons 3, 10, 17 and 23 (history, geography, culture, civics), a full section check with results, the journal, both print views, Educators, About, Settings and the 404.

## What was fixed

### Things that were wrong

- **An open glossary definition closed when Listen moved on.** The reading was re-keyed every time the highlight moved, so React replaced the glossary words: an open definition closed and keyboard focus fell to the page. Glossary words now keep their place in the page, and carry the highlight inside their own button when the sentence reaches them (`ReadingPassage.tsx`; a unit test and the Listen end-to-end test check it).
- **About's team cards had no padding.** They asked for `--space-7`, which the tokens don't have, so the browser dropped the rule. A new unit test (`src/styles/customProperties.test.ts`) checks that every `var(--name)` exists.
- **"What Thinkerwell is" on About was invisible**: a canvas card on a canvas page. It is a paper card with a border now, and About's cards use less padding on a phone.
- **The section check intro said every lesson was done** (a full "5/5" ring for a learner who had started none). It shows the learner's real count now, and no ring while looking around.
- **"Save and stop" in a section check saved nothing** (only a finished check is kept). It is "Stop the check".
- **"Only you see your score"** isn't true on a shared device. It is "Your score stays on this device", and the results say "Your score is saved on this device."
- **The course said "Section check not taken yet"** after the check had been taken. It says "The section check is ready."
- **The learner home said "Pick up where you left off" and "Continue"** for a lesson the learner had never opened. That is "Up next" and "Start" now.
- **Every video said "Captions not checked yet"**, the design reference's placeholder. The captions line shows only when captions are known.
- **Educators said "About 30 minutes a lesson"** while every lesson says 25 to 50; it shows the course page's range now. "Internet only for videos, once a lesson has opened" is "Internet for the first visit, and for videos".
- **Class codes were described as working now** ("Codes let us see how the whole group is doing"). Nothing is measured yet, so it says they *will*.
- **Settings suggested Edge for on-device Say it**; stable Edge can't (phase 5 notes).
- **The print view said the lesson prints in black and white**; its picture keeps its colours. It says the text prints black on white.

### Flow and layout

- **The big play circle on the video poster did nothing**; only the button under it did. A tap anywhere on the poster starts the player now. The button stays the one control for the keyboard and screen readers.
- **On a phone, "Who's learning today?" was a whole screen down**, under the laptop-sized hero. The hero uses the phone screen's sizes (52px tagline, 120px mascot).
- **On a tablet in portrait, a lone learner tile floated** in the middle of the left half of the panel. Tiles line up from the start, four to a row.
- **Long names broke inside words** ("Moham / med") in the phone tiles and the learner switcher. Those tiles give the name more room.
- **The not-found and error pages** had two small links on an empty page; they have the site's buttons now.
- **The quick check's back button said "Read"** although the learner is already in Read. It says "Part 3", like the parts before it.
- **Write's example answer**, opened before anything was written, was titled "Compare with an example answer". It says "See an example answer" until there is something to compare.
- **The "From Lesson N" link** in a section check had its icon in the middle of the link when it wrapped; it sits on the first line.
- **The "Pilot partners" tag** on Educators stretched across its card.
- **An open section on the course page** had a wide empty band before its first lesson.

### Words

The brand book asks for the product to speak as "we" only on About, for plain words, and for one word per thing. Changed in `src/i18n/messages/en.json`:

| Was | Now |
| --- | --- |
| What should we call you? / Tell us what to call you. | What's your name? / Type your name or a nickname. |
| Keep going; we'll try again. | Keep going: it tries again with your next change. |
| We can't find that page | This page isn't here |
| Go to home / Try reloading the page | Go to the home page / Try opening the page again |

| Your first guess | Your first idea |
| Choose a learner (look-around banner) | Choose who's learning (as everywhere else) |
| quiz results (removing a learner) | lesson work, journal, section check scores |

| Speak slowly; your words appear… | Speak slowly. Your words appear… |

| Step 1 of 1 (new-learner form) | removed |

### Link sharing

`index.html` had a description and a theme colour but no Open Graph or Twitter tags, so a pasted link showed a bare address. It now has both (title "Thinkerwell: Exploring Our World", one line, and a 1200 × 630 picture). The picture is the mascot, unchanged, on lemon above the wordmark and the course name, everything in the middle so a square preview still works. `scripts/make_social_card.mjs` draws it with the site's own fonts into `docs/design-system/assets/social-card.png`, and `scripts/optimise_images.py` makes the 32 kB copy the site serves (`public/social-card.png`). It isn't precached. The tags load nothing in a browser, so the Content-Security-Policy is unchanged. `src/app/linkPreview.test.ts` checks the tags and the picture. The addresses in the tags were `https://thinkerwell.vercel.app` until the domain was bought; they are now `https://thinkerwell.app`.

### Flaky tests

The whole Vitest suite was run three at a time, twice, to find tests that fail under load. Two were fixed:

- `JournalPage.test.tsx`, "edits an entry in place": right after Save it looked for the new words, but during the save the textbox itself holds them, so it sometimes found the textbox just before the save removed it. It now waits for the entry to leave editing.
- `LessonPlayerContext.test.tsx`, "waits for the typing pause": it checked that nothing was saved yet after two clicks, which on a busy machine can take longer than the 500 ms pause. The check now runs only when the test got there within the pause.

## Checked and left

- **"Watch the video" at about 644px.** Nothing covers the button at any width from 600 to 1024px: `elementFromPoint` at its centre and corners, scrolled every 40px, always hits the button, and a real mouse click at its centre opens the player. The likeliest cause of the earlier report is a click at a position measured before scrolling, or on the poster's play circle, which now works too.
- **Stage dots on the Educators preview list** are always empty (the rows are LessonRow, which draws them). Harmless, so left.
- **`[FEEDBACK EMAIL]`** on Educators is still a placeholder: it needs a real address from the team.
