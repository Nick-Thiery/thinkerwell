# Phase 7: section checks, journal, educators and about

Branch `phase-7-checks-and-pages`. Built in September 2026.

## What was built

- **Section checks** at `/section/:id/check` (`src/pages/SectionCheckPage.tsx`, `src/pages/quiz/`): an intro (what it covers, how long, that it can be retried, and whether this attempt will be saved), one question at a time with a progress bar and immediate feedback, a working "From Lesson N" link back to the question's own lesson, and results with the score, every missed question under "Worth another look" (each linking to its lesson), and "Try the check again". It is plain page state, not separate routes — nothing is ever locked, so restarting just resets the state. Options shuffle with `checkSeed`/`seededShuffle` (the same `src/lesson/shuffle.ts` the lesson player's quick checks use), keyed by learner id or `VISIT_SEED` while looking around.
- **Only a finished attempt is ever saved**, and only for a chosen learner (`store.recordQuizAttempt`, best and latest). "Save and stop" and every guest visit — looking around, or nobody chosen yet — leave nothing behind; a look-around learner can still take the whole check, just to see it.
- **Journal** at `/journal` (`src/pages/JournalPage.tsx`, `src/pages/journal/`): every piece of saved writing and every reflection for the current learner, newest first, grouped by lesson, with a filter (All / Writing / Reflections) and a "Print my journal" link to phase 6's `/journal/print`. Each entry (`EditableJournalEntry.tsx`) can be edited in place — Edit swaps it for a `WritingBox` with Save/Cancel, and Save writes straight back through `store.updateProgress`. A guest sees a note that nothing is saved; a chosen learner with nothing yet sees an empty state instead of an error.
- **Educators** at `/educators` (`src/pages/EducatorsPage.tsx`, `src/pages/educators/`): the hero, what a session needs, how it works, a section-filtered lesson list with a working preview link per lesson, a per-lesson disclosure for teaching notes and sources (closed by default), and a closing feedback card with `[FEEDBACK EMAIL]` as a plain placeholder — no form. Every lesson preview link carries `?preview=true`, so opening it never saves anything even when a real learner is already chosen on the device (`AppLayout` reads that query param for look-around mode on any route, regardless of who is chosen — the same mechanism phase 3 built for shared/kiosk devices).
- **About** at `/about` (`src/pages/AboutPage.tsx`): what Thinkerwell is, the promise to learners, the UN Sustainable Development Goals it works toward, and the team — Justin Park (Founder and CEO, real photo) and Nick Thiery (CTO, a placeholder "Photo coming" panel).
- **The dashboard's section-check count is real**: `LearnerDashboard.tsx` now reads the actual question count from `getQuiz(section.id)` instead of a placeholder constant.
- **Two Playwright tests**: `e2e/section-check.spec.ts` plays a full check end to end (intro, ten questions, one deliberately wrong, results, the saved attempt, "Try again"), and `e2e/journal.spec.ts` writes an answer and a reflection, then checks the journal groups, filters, edits and persists them, ending on the print view showing the edited text.

## Decisions

1. **`JournalPiece` gained a `reflectionIndex`** (`src/storage/progress.ts`) so an edit made in the journal writes back to the exact reflection it came from, not just "some reflection for this lesson".
2. **The journal keeps its own `overrides` state** layered over `useLearnerProgress`'s snapshot, merged with `useMemo`. That hook loads once per learner and has no built-in refetch, and re-fetching the whole learner on every keystroke-adjacent save would be wasteful; a small local override is enough to show an edit immediately and is dropped on the next real load (e.g. a reload, as the e2e test checks).
3. **Educators' "Lesson plans (PDF)" becomes "See a lesson on paper"**, linking to an existing lesson's `/print` view (phase 6). The mock names a PDF download that doesn't exist and wasn't asked for elsewhere; a real, working link to a real printable lesson serves the same need (something to hand an educator on paper) without inventing a feature.
4. **Every top-level page's visible `<h1>` matches its page title exactly.** This is a pre-existing, tested contract (`src/app/routes.test.tsx`), and two of the new screens' mocks put a marketing headline in the `<h1>` position instead: Educators' "Run a Thinkerwell lesson with your group", and the section check's "What do you remember from {section}?". Both are now a styled subtitle directly under the real `<h1>` ("For educators", "Section check: {section}"), not dropped — `docs/screens/README.md`'s own tie-breaker ("if a screen and the design-system docs disagree, the docs win") applies here just as much to this pre-existing app-wide convention.
5. **`SectionCheckPage` no longer waits for the learner session to load before showing anything.** An earlier draft blocked on `session.status === 'loading'` to avoid reshuffling a question's options mid-answer if the seed changed under it. The intro screen doesn't depend on the session at all, and the only screen that does (questions, which need a seed) is reached only after "Start the check" — by which point the session has always long since resolved. The guard just meant nothing rendered on first paint.
6. **The section check's phone-viewport Playwright test forces its "Next question" clicks** (`e2e/section-check.spec.ts`, `clickNext`). On the `phone` project only, once a question's feedback pushes the ActionBar below the fold, Chromium's mobile-viewport emulation misreports `window.innerHeight` (and everything derived from it) at roughly 1.4x the real 390x844 viewport. Playwright's actionability check clips its click point to the real, correctly-sized viewport and lands on the feedback card instead of the button it just scrolled to. This was checked thoroughly: a screenshot at the moment of "failure" shows the button fully visible with no overlap, and `document.elementFromPoint` at the button's own computed centre consistently finds the button throughout the whole retry window — a forced click lands correctly and the check advances as expected. `force: true` skips only that misled visibility check; it is scoped to this one helper, not the shared `nextButton`, since every other spec using it (including the lesson player's own phone-width tests, with the same `QuestionCard`/`Feedback`/`ActionBar`) passes without it.

## Not done, or for later

- The Educators page's teaching-notes disclosure is per lesson and closed by default, matching `docs/PRODUCT.md`'s note that `educatorNotes` and `sources` reach the production bundle "for the Educators page" — this phase is what fulfils that.
- As with phase 6, everything above was checked in headless Chromium via Playwright against a production build, not on a real phone or tablet.
