# ContinueCard

The big lemon card at the top of the dashboard that takes the learner back to their next step.

Props: `eyebrow`, `title` (lesson title), `lessonLabel`, `done`, `current`, `stageLabel` ("Next: Write"), `cta`, `href`.

- One per dashboard, always first. For a brand-new learner it says "Start here" and points at Lesson 1.
