# SegmentedControl

Two to four mutually exclusive options in one pill, such as Standard | Simpler English or Write | Starters | Plan.

Props: `options` (strings or `{label, value, icon}`), `value`, `onChange`, `label` (accessible group name).

- The reading level switch is always "Standard" and "Simpler"; it keeps the learner's place.
- Do not use for navigation between pages.
