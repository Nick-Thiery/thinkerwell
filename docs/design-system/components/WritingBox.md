# WritingBox

A labelled text area for writing and reflections, with a helper line.

Props: `id`, `label`, `optional`, `placeholder`, `value`, `rows`, `helper`, `dictate` (true shows a "Say it" `VoiceButton` beside the label; 'listening' shows it recording).

- The label is the prompt. The helper says how much is enough ("4–6 sentences is plenty"), never a word-count gate.
- Writing saves on the device as the learner types.
- With `dictate`, spoken words are added at the cursor as a draft the learner can edit.
