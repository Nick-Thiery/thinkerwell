# Art, Architecture and Society, Malay (ms): terms beyond KEY_TERMS.md

Two AI translators (course.json and Lessons 1–10; Lessons 11–20) share this list so the course uses one word for one idea. Add rows; don't overwrite or rewrite other rows. `docs/translation/ms/KEY_TERMS.md` comes first, then Southeast Asia Through Time's Malay `TERMS.md` (*bukti*, *sumber*, *konteks*, *dakwaan*, *muzium*, *kapsyen*, *kredit*, *warisan*, *pemuliharaan*). Standard Malaysian Malay (DBP spelling), never Indonesian. All of it is for a native reviewer to confirm.

**Pictures:** both pictures in every lesson are photographs of real works and stay as they are. Their `alt` and `caption` are translated; `description` and `credit` stay English. Artwork titles stay in their original language (as the museum gives them), with a short Malay gloss in brackets where it helps, usually the first time in the reading; not repeated in the simpler text.
