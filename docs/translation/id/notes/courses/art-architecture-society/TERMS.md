# Art, Architecture and Society in Bahasa Indonesia: terms beyond KEY_TERMS.md

Two translators work on the course at the same time (course.json and Lessons 1–10; Lessons 11–20). Read this file before choosing a term, and add to it (never overwrite or rewrite the file: append rows). KEY_TERMS.md and Southeast Asia Through Time's TERMS.md come first: *bukti*, *sumber*, *konteks*, *Tiongkok* (the country) and *Tionghoa* (people), *Benar.* / *Belum tepat.*, *kamu* for learners, *aku* when the learner speaks, and no "Anda" in lesson files (teachers' notes use imperatives or no pronoun; `scripts/check_translation.py id` rejects "Anda").

**Pictures:** both pictures in every lesson are photographs of real works and stay as they are. Their `alt` and `caption` are translated; `description` and `credit` stay English (as Southeast Asia's `secondVisual.credit`). Artwork titles in captions stay as the museum gives them.
